# Industrial decision-support safety policy

tags: safety, approval, industrial, governance

ForgeGuard is an auxiliary decision-support system. It does not directly control production equipment.
Actions that stop production, alter a production plan, release a machine after maintenance, or accept
continued operation under high risk require an authorized human approver and an auditable record.

When evidence is missing, contradictory, out of distribution, or below its quality threshold, the
system must request additional evidence or escalate rather than invent a certain conclusion.
