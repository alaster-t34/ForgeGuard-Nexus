# Rolling-element bearing FMEA

tags: bearing, FMEA, vibration, spalling, lubrication

## Localized raceway spalling

Typical supporting evidence includes impulsive vibration, elevated kurtosis and crest factor,
repetitive impact components related to bearing defect frequencies, and localized surface damage.
A single classifier output is not sufficient for a safety-relevant maintenance decision.

## Lubrication degradation

Typical supporting evidence includes temperature rise, broadband vibration growth, acoustic change,
and recent lubrication history. Visual evidence may remain inconclusive during early degradation.

## Evidence conflict rule

When sensor quality is degraded or independent modalities disagree, validate acquisition integrity,
repeat measurements under known speed and load, and request human review before intervention.
