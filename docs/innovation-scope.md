# Publicly explainable innovation scope

## Existing assets retained

The user-provided RV1126B bearing project contains valuable acquisition, WDCNN classification, FP-FLNet RUL, ONNX/RKNN visual inference, and offline analysis assets. ForgeGuard does not claim those third-party weights or public datasets as newly invented. They are connected through an optional legacy adapter.

## Newly developed in ForgeGuard Nexus

1. **Evidence-first industrial agent state machine.** Every diagnosis, plan, approval, work order, and verification result is represented by typed data and linked evidence.
2. **Modality and hardware neutrality.** The edge contract accepts vibration, images, acoustic, temperature, current, and future modalities without binding the platform to RV1126B or IIS3DWB.
3. **Model portfolio governance.** Supervised defect models, visual anomaly models, vibration foundation models, and RUL models are adapters selected by benchmark and deployment constraints rather than hard-coded into the business flow.
4. **Contradiction-aware acquisition.** Disagreement between modalities or poor data quality produces a new evidence request instead of a fabricated certain diagnosis.
5. **RUL uncertainty as a business constraint.** The platform uses p10/p50/p90 intervals and continuation risk, not a single unqualified number.
6. **Maintenance decision arena.** Multiple options are ranked with safety, downtime, cost, inventory, skills, and production context.
7. **Proof-carrying work orders.** Approval and closure are coupled to an auditable evidence bundle and before/after verification.
8. **Policy-controlled search.** Agents can retrieve broad external knowledge through an allowlisted, read-only search broker with citation retention and query-purpose logging.
9. **Reproducible dual path.** The deterministic path proves the complete system without a paid API; optional frontier models can enhance perception and explanation while the safety gates remain deterministic.

## Claims we deliberately do not make

- No claim that a single model is universally best.
- No claim that synthetic demo accuracy represents plant-wide performance.
- No claim that RUL estimates are certified safety guarantees.
- No claim that the system replaces maintenance engineers or safety officers.
